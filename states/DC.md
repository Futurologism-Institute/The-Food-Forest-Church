# District of Columbia — edible native plants

Mid-Atlantic urban/riparian flora shared with Maryland and Virginia.

## Sources
- [University of Maryland Extension](https://extension.umd.edu/) / [Virginia Cooperative Extension](https://ext.vt.edu/) — regional guidance
- [USDA Plants — District of Columbia](https://plants.usda.gov/)
- [Lady Bird Johnson Wildflower Center — DC](https://www.wildflower.org/plants/search.php?state=DC)
- Local native plant societies and park naturalists

## Examples often cited
Serviceberry, pawpaw (where present), wild strawberry, black walnut, native blackberries/raspberries (*Rubus*).
