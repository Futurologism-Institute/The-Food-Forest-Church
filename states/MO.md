# Missouri — edible native plants

Ozarks, prairie remnants, river hills. Strong native-plant and specialty-crop outreach.

## Sources that cite edible natives

- [Lincoln University Native Plants Program / specialty crops](https://www.lincolnu.edu/) — work by Nadia Navarrete-Tindall and colleagues on native edibles
- [University of Missouri Extension](https://extension.missouri.edu/)
- [Grow Native! (Missouri Prairie Foundation)](https://grownative.org/)
- [USDA Plants — Missouri](https://plants.usda.gov/)
- [Lady Bird Johnson Wildflower Center — Missouri](https://www.wildflower.org/plants/search.php?state=MO)
- Prairie ethnobotany (e.g. Kindscher’s *Edible Wild Plants of the Prairie*)

## Example species often cited

- Wild plum (*Prunus americana* and related)
- Serviceberry (*Amelanchier*)
- Pawpaw (*Asimina triloba*)
- Wild onion / prairie onion (*Allium* spp.)
- Groundnut / hopniss (*Apios americana*)
- Persimmon (*Diospyros virginiana*)
