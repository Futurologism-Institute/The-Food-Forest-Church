# Alaska — edible native plants

Boreal forest, tundra, coastal rainforest, Aleutians.

## Sources

- [University of Alaska Fairbanks Cooperative Extension](https://www.uaf.edu/ces/)
- [USDA Plants — Alaska](https://plants.usda.gov/)
- [Lady Bird Johnson Wildflower Center — Alaska](https://www.wildflower.org/plants/search.php?state=AK)
- Local foraging and ethnobotany guides (berries, seaweeds, greens)

## Example species often cited

- Blueberries and related *Vaccinium*
- Salmonberry (*Rubus spectabilis*)
- Crowberry (*Empetrum nigrum*)
- Fireweed (*Chamerion angustifolium*) — young shoots, flowers
- Beach greens / coastal species (identify carefully)
