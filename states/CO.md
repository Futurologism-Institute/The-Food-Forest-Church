# Colorado — edible native plants

Rockies, high plains, plateaus.

## Sources

- [Colorado State University Extension](https://extension.colostate.edu/)
- [USDA Plants — Colorado](https://plants.usda.gov/)
- [Lady Bird Johnson Wildflower Center — Colorado](https://www.wildflower.org/plants/search.php?state=CO)
- Regional Rocky Mountain foraging and ethnobotany references

## Example species often cited

- Serviceberry (*Amelanchier alnifolia* and related)
- Chokecherry (*Prunus virginiana*) — fruit (pits toxic)
- Wild strawberry (*Fragaria* spp.)
- Pine nuts from native pines (species-dependent; check legality and ecology)
- Currants and gooseberries (*Ribes* spp.)
