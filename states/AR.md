# Arkansas — edible native plants

Ozarks, Ouachitas, Delta, Gulf coastal plain edge.

## Sources

- [University of Arkansas System Division of Agriculture — Cooperative Extension](https://www.uaex.uada.edu/)
- [USDA Plants — Arkansas](https://plants.usda.gov/)
- [Lady Bird Johnson Wildflower Center — Arkansas](https://www.wildflower.org/plants/search.php?state=AR)

## Example species often cited

- Pawpaw (*Asimina triloba*)
- Persimmon (*Diospyros virginiana*)
- Muscadine / native grapes (*Vitis* spp.)
- Blackberries and related *Rubus*
- Hickory nuts (*Carya* spp.)
