# Hawaii — edible native plants

Note: Hawaiian flora is unique; many “common” edibles are Polynesian introductions. Focus here on **endemic/indigenous** species with documented food use, plus careful cultural context.

## Sources
- [University of Hawaiʻi Cooperative Extension](https://www.ctahr.hawaii.edu/site/ExtPrograms.aspx)
- [USDA Plants — Hawaii](https://plants.usda.gov/)
- Native Hawaiian ethnobotany references (e.g. traditional uses of ʻuala, kalo are culturally central but not all are “native” in the continental sense)

## Examples
Seek local cultural practitioners and extension for indigenous edible species and protocols. Avoid treating culturally restricted plants as generic forage.
