# New York — edible native plants

Northeast forests, Hudson Valley, Adirondacks, Great Lakes plain, Long Island coastal.

## Sources that cite edible natives

- [Cornell Cooperative Extension](https://cce.cornell.edu/) — county educators; native plants and food
- [New York Flora Atlas](https://newyork.plantatlas.usf.edu/) — distribution
- [USDA Plants — New York](https://plants.usda.gov/)
- [Lady Bird Johnson Wildflower Center — New York](https://www.wildflower.org/plants/search.php?state=NY)
- Northeast foraging references (e.g. beach plum, wineberry context, woodland understory)

## Example species often cited

- Serviceberry / Juneberry (*Amelanchier* spp.)
- Highbush blueberry (*Vaccinium corymbosum*)
- Wild strawberry (*Fragaria virginiana*)
- Black cherry (*Prunus serotina*) — fruit (pits/leaves toxic)
- Beach plum (*Prunus maritima*) — coastal
- Ramps / wild leek (*Allium tricoccum*) — harvest sustainably; often overharvested

Ramps and other spring ephemerals need careful, ethical harvest limits.
