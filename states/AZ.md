# Arizona — edible native plants

Sonoran and Mojave deserts, sky islands, Colorado Plateau.

## Sources

- [University of Arizona Cooperative Extension](https://extension.arizona.edu/)
- *Edible and Useful Plants of the Southwest* (Delena Tull) — covers Arizona
- [USDA Plants — Arizona](https://plants.usda.gov/)
- [Lady Bird Johnson Wildflower Center — Arizona](https://www.wildflower.org/plants/search.php?state=AZ)
- Native Seeds/SEARCH and regional ethnobotany resources

## Example species often cited

- Saguaro (*Carnegiea gigantea*) — fruit (cultural harvest norms apply)
- Prickly pear (*Opuntia* spp.)
- Mesquite (*Prosopis* spp.) — pods
- Cholla buds (proper preparation required)
- Jojoba (*Simmondsia chinensis*) — seeds (oil; not a bulk food)
