# Texas — edible native plants

Huge range: Piney Woods, Prairies, Hill Country, South Texas Plains, Trans-Pecos desert.

## Sources that cite edible natives

- [Texas A&M AgriLife Extension](https://agrilifeextension.tamu.edu/) — county agents; native plant and food programs
- [Lady Bird Johnson Wildflower Center](https://www.wildflower.org/) (Austin) — native plant database and recommendations
- *Edible and Useful Plants of the Southwest* (Delena Tull, University of Texas Press) — Texas, New Mexico, Arizona
- [USDA Plants — Texas](https://plants.usda.gov/)
- [Native Plant Society of Texas](https://www.npsot.org/)

## Example species often cited

- Prickly pear (*Opuntia engelmannii* and related) — pads and fruit
- Mesquite (*Prosopis glandulosa*) — pods/flour
- Agarita (*Mahonia trifoliolata*) — berries
- Texas persimmon (*Diospyros texana*)
- Pecans (*Carya illinoinensis*) — native range includes Texas
- Passionflower / maypop (*Passiflora incarnata*) in eastern parts

Cross-check ecoregion; West Texas and East Texas plant lists differ sharply.
