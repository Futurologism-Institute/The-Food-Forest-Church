# Alabama — edible native plants

Southeast forests, coastal plain, Appalachian foothills.

## Sources

- [Alabama Cooperative Extension System](https://www.aces.edu/)
- [Alabama Wildflower Society](https://www.alawildflowers.org/) (or regional native plant groups)
- [USDA Plants — Alabama](https://plants.usda.gov/)
- [Lady Bird Johnson Wildflower Center — Alabama](https://www.wildflower.org/plants/search.php?state=AL)

## Example species often cited

- Pawpaw (*Asimina triloba*)
- Persimmon (*Diospyros virginiana*)
- Native blueberries (*Vaccinium* spp.)
- Passionflower / maypop (*Passiflora incarnata*)
- Hickory and oak (nuts; process properly)
