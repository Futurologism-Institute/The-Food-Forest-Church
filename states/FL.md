# Florida — edible native plants

Subtropical to warm-temperate. Many coastal and inland natives with documented food uses.

## Sources that cite edible natives

- [Florida’s Edible Native Plants — UF/IFAS Gardening Solutions](https://gardeningsolutions.ifas.ufl.edu/plants/edibles/floridas-edible-native-plants/) — University of Florida list (beautyberry, blueberry, cabbage palm, etc.)
- [UF/IFAS Extension](https://sfyl.ifas.ufl.edu/) — county offices; ask about native food plants
- [Florida Native Plant Society](https://www.fnps.org/) — native plant resources by region
- [USDA Plants — Florida](https://plants.usda.gov/) — filter by state + growth habit
- [Lady Bird Johnson Wildflower Center — Florida](https://www.wildflower.org/plants/search.php?search_field=&newsearch=true&state=FL)

## Example species often cited

- Beautyberry (*Callicarpa americana*)
- Native blueberries (*Vaccinium* spp.)
- Cabbage palm / sabal (*Sabal palmetto*) — heart (harvest with extreme care; can kill the palm)
- Coontie (*Zamia integrifolia*) — historically processed starch (requires proper detoxification; toxic if mishandled)
- Sea grape (*Coccoloba uvifera*) — fruit

Always verify identification and preparation with extension or a trained botanist.
