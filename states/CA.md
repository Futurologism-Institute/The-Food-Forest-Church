# California — edible native plants

Mediterranean coast, Central Valley, Sierra, deserts. Extremely high floristic diversity; many ethnobotanical records.

## Sources that cite edible natives

- [California Native Plant Society](https://www.cnps.org/) — native plant resources and chapters
- [UC Cooperative Extension](https://ucanr.edu/) — local advisors; search “native edible” or “foraging”
- [USDA Plants — California](https://plants.usda.gov/)
- [Lady Bird Johnson Wildflower Center — California](https://www.wildflower.org/plants/search.php?state=CA)
- Regional ethnobotany and foraging literature (e.g. works covering acorns, manzanita, mesquite, coastal berries)

## Example species often cited

- Coast live oak / other oaks (*Quercus* spp.) — acorns (leaching required)
- Manzanita (*Arctostaphylos* spp.) — berries, cider
- California blackberry / related *Rubus*
- Miner’s lettuce (*Claytonia perfoliata*)
- Chia (*Salvia columbariae*) — seeds
- Prickly pear (*Opuntia* spp.) where native

Identify carefully; many look-alikes exist across California’s ecoregions.
